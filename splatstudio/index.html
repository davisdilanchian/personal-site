Splat Studio

Turn a video into a 3D Gaussian splat.

About

Walk around something with your phone or Meta glasses, and Splat Studio turns the video into a 3D Gaussian splat you can view and export.

How it works

  • Frames: frames are pulled out of the video at a steady rate.
  • Camera poses: structure-from-motion works out where the camera was for each frame.
  • Training: a 3D Gaussian splat is trained from the frames and poses.
  • Export: .ply for Blender and SuperSplat, or .splat and .ksplat for web viewers.

Built with

  • Mac
  • Structure from motion
  • 3D Gaussian Splatting
