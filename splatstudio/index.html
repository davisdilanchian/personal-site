OPERATIONAL

EXHIBIT: SPLAT STUDIO
▌ Overview

Walk around something with your phone or Meta glasses, then run gm run capture.MOV. Splat Studio pulls frames out of the video, works out where the camera was for each one, trains a 3D Gaussian splat, and exports it ready to view.

The pipeline

  • Frames: FFmpeg pulls frames at a set rate (2 per second by default, up to 300).
  • Camera poses: COLMAP, or GLOMAP for a 10 to 50x speed-up.
  • Training: Nerfstudio splatfacto on gsplat by default; OpenSplat for AMD and Apple GPUs.
  • Export: .ply for Blender and SuperSplat, .splat and .ksplat for web viewers.

Quality presets run from low to extreme, and gm check tells you what is installed before you start.

▌ Known Capabilities
  • Python CLI
  • COLMAP / GLOMAP
  • Nerfstudio + gsplat
  • 3D Gaussian Splatting
