EXHIBIT: STAFFWISE
▌ Overview

Staffwise drills two piano skills: reading notes off the staff fast, and hearing music and finding it on the keys. Answer on the on-screen keyboard, or switch to My piano and play your real piano; Staffwise listens through the microphone and works out the notes on your iPhone after a 30-second calibration.

Sight reading

A note or chord appears on the staff; play it as fast as you can. Notes you miss or answer slowly come up more often until they are quick.

Ear School

Hear a note, chord or short phrase and find it on the keys. Listening comes before testing, and old material keeps coming back for review, borrowed from the Suzuki method.

Free and Plus

Practice is free. The free version shows a few ads, never during practice. Staffwise Plus is a one-time purchase that removes them and syncs your progress through your own iCloud. Your practice history stays on your iPhone, and the microphone is never recorded.
