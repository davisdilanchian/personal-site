EXHIBIT: GROUPIER
▌ Overview

Put two axes of anything on a board (days by times, places by dates) and send the link. Everyone gets the same number of chips and spends them on the squares they actually want. The top square with everyone on it wins. No accounts.

The rules

  • Identical chips. The creator picks how many; there are no denominations.
  • A chip is worth 1 on the square it sits on. Betting a whole row or column puts a chip on every square in it, so flexibility is possible but costs more.
  • Saying you can't make something is always free. Chips express preference; feasibility is not for sale.
  • Anything can be ruled out (the place is shut, the tickets are gone). Chips on it are handed back.
