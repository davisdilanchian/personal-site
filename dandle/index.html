Dandle

A 3D word-building physics game.

Play at dandle.online

About

Dandle is a word game with physics. Each level starts with a random word laid out as 3D letter cubes on a checkerboard floor. Click a cube, type a word that contains that letter, and the new word branches off at a right angle, building a crossword-like structure.

Verbs are different: they apply force in the direction they are placed, shown by red arrows. Use them to push the whole structure into the red end zone to finish the level.

Controls

  • Drag to orbit the camera, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.
  • Click a letter cube to select it, then type a word and press Enter.

It runs in the browser with Three.js for the 3D and procedural sound from the Web Audio API.

Built with

  • Three.js
  • Real-time physics
  • Level editor
  • WebGL
