PRIVACY POLICY
8mb — Photo & Video Compressor

Privacy Policy

Effective: 24 June 2026

8mb is private by design. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no account system, no servers, and no third-party analytics, and it makes no network connections.

Photos & Videos

8mb uses your photo library only to (1) read the photo or video you choose and (2) save the compressed copy back. All compression happens entirely on your device — nothing is uploaded for processing. Your original file is never modified.

Data We Collect

None. 8mb stores no personal information and sends nothing to us or any third party. It contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

Children

8mb does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
