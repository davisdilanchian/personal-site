PRIVACY POLICY
8mb — Photo & Video Compressor

Privacy Policy

Effective: 3 October 2026

8mb compresses your photos and videos entirely on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded to us or anyone else. The free version shows ads, provided by Google AdMob. A one-time purchase removes them.

Photos & Videos

8mb uses your photo library only to (1) read the photo or video you choose and (2) save the compressed copy back. All compression happens entirely on your device. Your original file is never modified. Ad providers never receive your photos or videos.

Advertising

The free version of 8mb shows ads from Google AdMob in the main app. (The Share extension never shows ads.) To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • Tracking permission: 8mb asks once whether apps may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", Google does not receive your advertising identifier and ads are less personalized. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: 8mb asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time with "Privacy choices" on the main screen.

Purchases

"Remove Ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. 8mb never sees your payment details. After you buy it, the app no longer loads the ad SDK, and no ad data is collected.

Data We Collect

We (the developer) collect nothing. 8mb has no account system and no servers of its own. The only data collection is by Google AdMob, as described above, in the free version.

Children

8mb is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
