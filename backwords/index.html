FIELD TEST

▸ View on GitHub
EXHIBIT — BACKWORDS
▌ Overview

Type a line and Backwords spells it out reversed, so you can say it into a mic, flip the recording, and hear the original line come out. You learn it by hearing the line reversed and copying it; the spelling is only a crutch.

The spelling is learned from you, not from a speech synthesiser. A calibration round plays short pieces backwards and asks how you would write down what you hear. The pieces are ordered by how much work they do, and the first fifty cover about half of everything you will ever type.

▌ Known Capabilities
  • Web Audio
  • Phonetic reversal
  • Personal spelling calibration
  • Runs in the browser
