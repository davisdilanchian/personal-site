Risographer

OPERATIONAL

A browser tool that makes photos look risograph-printed.

▸ Open Risographer ▸ View on GitHub
EXHIBIT — RISOGRAPHER
▌ Overview

Drop images into one of two ink folders (blue and pink by default), then move and rotate them around a Letter, A3 or custom sheet.

The halftone screen is anchored to the page, not to each image. That is how real riso works: the screen belongs to the plate, not the artwork. Images slide through a fixed dot grid, two images on the same ink share one continuous screen, and the two inks multiply where they cross. Dot pitch is set in lines per inch so the print looks the same at any export resolution.

It is one HTML file. No install, no account, nothing uploaded.

▌ Known Capabilities
  • Canvas rendering
  • Page-anchored halftone
  • Two-ink overprint
  • Client-side only
