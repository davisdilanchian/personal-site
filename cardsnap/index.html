CardSnap

A business-card scanner that reads every field.

About

An iOS business-card scanner. It captures automatically when the card is in focus, extracts every contact field plus handwritten notes with Gemini AI, and exports to JSON/CSV.

Built with

  • SwiftUI
  • AVFoundation
  • Vision (quality detection)
  • Gemini AI
  • JSON / CSV export
