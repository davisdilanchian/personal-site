PRIVACY POLICY
Keywise: Piano Sight & Ear

Privacy Policy

Effective: 9 October 2026

Keywise trains you to read piano notes and recognise them by ear. Your practice history stays on your iPhone. We (the developer) have no servers and collect nothing. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. A one-time purchase, Keywise Plus, removes them.

Practice data

Your answers, times and progress are saved on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to Google. You can export a backup file from Settings; where you keep it is up to you.

Microphone

Using the microphone is optional. If you choose "My piano", Keywise listens to the notes you play and analyses the sound on your iPhone, as it happens. The sound is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The only exception is the optional benchmark recorder under Settings › Developer, which saves recordings as files on your iPhone only, when you start it yourself. The on-screen keys work without the microphone.

iCloud sync (Keywise Plus)

With Keywise Plus, your practice history is also copied to your own private iCloud so it follows you to another iPhone. Only your Apple ID can read it; we cannot. You can turn sync off in Settings.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob on the Home and Progress screens and, occasionally, after a finished session. Ads never appear during practice. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Your practice history is never shared with Google.

  • Tracking permission: Keywise asks once whether apps may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", Google does not receive your advertising identifier and ads are not personalised. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: Keywise asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time with "Ad privacy choices" in Keywise's Settings.

Purchases

Keywise Plus is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. Keywise never sees your payment details. After you buy it, the app makes no ad requests.

Data we collect

None. Keywise has no account system, no analytics of its own and no servers. The only data collection is by Google AdMob, as described above, in the free version.

Children

Keywise is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
