How Keywise works

  • Sight reading: a note or chord appears on the staff; play it on the keys as fast as you can.
  • Ear School: hear a note, chord or short phrase and find it on the keys.
  • It drills your weak spots: notes you miss or answer slowly come up more often until they are quick.
  • Your own piano: optionally answer on a real piano through the microphone.

FAQ

Can I use my real piano?

Yes. In Settings, choose "My piano" and run the short calibration (about 30 seconds). You can switch between Keys and Piano during practice. A quiet room works best.

What does Keywise Plus include?

A one-time purchase that removes ads and syncs your progress through your own iCloud. Practice itself is free either way. Use "Restore Purchases" in Settings on a new iPhone.

How do I keep my progress safe?

Settings › Backup exports a file you can keep anywhere and import later. With Keywise Plus, iCloud sync also keeps a copy in your iCloud.

Is my data private?

Your practice history stays on your iPhone (and your own iCloud with Plus). The microphone is analysed on the iPhone and never recorded. See the Privacy Policy.
