Jack-o

OPERATIONAL

Turn an old phone into a talking jack-o'-lantern.

▸ Open jack-o.io ▸ GitHub
EXHIBIT: JACK-O
▌ Overview

Put an old phone in a pumpkin and Jack-o turns it into a jack-o'-lantern that hears trick-or-treaters and talks back in character: a smug pumpkin, a cackling witch, a vampire and more. It answers with a live AI voice and flickers the phone's flashlight and screen as it talks.

Setting it up

  1. Open jack-o.io on an old phone.
  2. Power it up: enter a free Google key, get a Pumpkin Pass, or scan one from your other phone.
  3. Press Start and put the phone in the pumpkin, screen facing out.

Free or paid

It is free with your own Google key, with each character in its own voice. A one-time $1.99 unlock adds any voice for any character, characters you write yourself, and lantern colors. A $4.99 Pumpkin Pass needs no key at all and covers about 400 replies with every voice and color.

Your key stays on your phone and is only sent to Google. Flashlight control works best in Chrome on Android.
