PRIVACY POLICY
Mugula: Disappearing Photos

Privacy Policy

Effective: 5 October 2026

Mugula works without creating a Mugula account. Editing happens on your device. Normal Mugula delivery encrypts your media before upload. We use limited referral analytics to understand how people discover Mugula through messages from friends. Mugula does not show ads, sell referral data, or share it with advertisers.

Photos & Camera

Mugula uses your camera and photo library only so you can capture or choose a photo or video to edit. All editing (drawing, stickers, captions, and filters) happens entirely on your device.

Face Data

Some Mugula camera effects (face filters and face-warp effects) need to know where a face is on screen in order to place a sticker or distortion correctly.

  • What is collected: Mugula uses Apple's on-device Vision framework (VNDetectFaceLandmarksRequest) to compute the on-screen positions of facial landmarks (the outlines of eyes, nose, and mouth) for the current camera frame or the photo you are editing.
  • How it is used: solely to draw the visual effect you selected. It is used for positioning graphics only.
  • No identification: Mugula does not perform face recognition or identity matching, and does not create, derive, or store a faceprint, face template, or any other biometric identifier.
  • Where it is processed: entirely on your device. Face data is never transmitted off the device, never uploaded to our servers or to iCloud, and is never shared with any third party.
  • Retention: none. Landmark positions exist only in memory while the frame is being drawn and are discarded immediately afterward, typically within a fraction of a second. They are never written to disk. Only the finished picture or video you choose to send is saved or sent; the underlying face data is not included in it.

How Your Media Is Sent

  • When you send a photo or video, it is encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM) before it leaves. The encrypted file is delivered through Apple iCloud (CloudKit). The decryption key travels only inside the end-to-end-encrypted message, not with the stored file, so only your recipient can open it. We cannot read your media.
  • Media is temporary. It automatically deletes after the sender's chosen availability window (default 48 hours), and "view once" media is removed as soon as it is viewed. Deletion is client-driven: the encrypted iCloud copy is removed the next time a participating device opens Mugula after the window passes.
  • Compatibility mode (an option you turn on per send) instead sends your media as a standard message attachment so any recipient can view it. In this mode the media is not encrypted by Mugula and does not auto-delete. The app tells you this before you turn it on.

Referral Analytics and Identifiers

Mugula creates a random, app-specific identifier on your device. The sending device's identifier is included in each Mugula message. When a newly observed recipient successfully opens a received photo or video, Mugula can send the sending and receiving devices' identifiers, the first-view time, the reporting week and month, and the referral format version to Apple CloudKit. We use this to measure how Mugula spreads and avoid counting the same recipient repeatedly. These records measure first views, not verified downloads or verified people.

These identifiers are pseudonymous, not anonymous. They contain no name, phone number, email address, advertising identifier, or hardware address. Mugula does not read your address book, message text, or location. A referral record links a sending and receiving device. CloudKit also stores normal record timestamps and an app-scoped creator reference. Referral records are separate from the encrypted media and its decryption keys.

The identifier and credited referral are stored on your device, including in Keychain when available, and may survive reinstallation. Referral records are retained for historical reporting until removed by the developer. They have no automatic deletion schedule and are not subject to photo or video expiry timers.

We do not combine this information with data from other companies for targeted advertising or advertising measurement, and do not share it with data brokers.

Media Metadata and Capture Events

CloudKit stores the encrypted file, a random media identifier, whether it is a photo or video, creation time, and CloudKit's record metadata. Where screenshot or screen-recording detection is available, Mugula may store the event type and time linked to the media identifier so the sender can be told. This does not upload the screenshot or recording. Detection cannot prevent someone from making a separate copy of your media.

CloudKit is provided by Apple. Apple processes service and account information under its own privacy terms. We do not receive your Apple Account email address or password.

Saved Filters, Drafts and Face Swap

Saved filters, drafts and selected Face Swap photographs are stored locally. Camera landmarks and Face Swap correspondence stay on the device. Saved sculpt filters store the warp settings you create, not a biometric identity. Filter files leave your device only when you choose to share them. Edited photos and videos include the visible result when you choose to save or send them.

Data Retention

Encrypted media stored in iCloud (CloudKit) is pruned at its availability expiry (default 48 hours, or sooner for view-once media). Cleanup runs when participating devices use Mugula; deletion can be delayed while devices are offline or the app is not running. Local cached copies on your device are removed on the same per-send expiry; any local file without a recorded expiry is cleared after a 14-day fallback limit at the latest. You can shorten this or clear the cache at any time from the Mugula app's Storage settings.

Your Choices and Requests

You can delete local drafts and saved filters from their galleries and clear media caches from Storage settings. This does not remove copies that recipients saved or standard attachments sent with Compatibility mode. To request removal of referral records, email ddilanchian@gmail.com with the Mugula ID shown in Storage and Privacy settings. Do not send us your private media or decryption keys.

Children

Mugula is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes
